# Validation Design
## 1. Question
Does Shortlink reduce broken-link helpdesk tickets?
## 2. Hypotheses
H-1: tickets/month at 3 months after launch ≤ 70% of the P0 baseline.
## 4. Data
Helpdesk tickets tagged "broken link", 2 months before launch and months 1–3 after.
## 7. Pre-registered success criteria
`policy:success.ticket_reduction` = 0.30, judged on month 3 alone.
## 9. Threats to validity
Seasonal ticket volume; tagging drift. Mitigation: the same triage team tags tickets in both periods.
